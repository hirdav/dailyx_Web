Privacy Policy

DailyX is a small, calm app for tasks, reminders and a short daily journal. This page explains, in plain words, what the app stores about you, why, and what you can do about it. DailyX is provided by Edantra.

The short version

  • You need an account, so your tasks and journal can follow you between sessions.
  • Your entries are stored on a secure database and are private to your account.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and everything in it at any time.

What DailyX stores

  • Account details: your email address, your name (if you give one), a password (stored by the sign-in service only as a secure hash, never in readable form), or your Google account's name and email if you sign in with Google.
  • Your content: tasks, subtasks, dates, repeat settings and reminders; completions and the XP you earn; journal entries with your mood and any text you write; and the small history and statistics the app builds from these, such as your consistency score, your year heatmap and the trees in your Grove.
  • Preferences: your profile picture choice, dark mode setting and reminder times.

Mood and journal entries can be personal. They are treated like the rest of your content: stored for you, and not used for anything else.

Why we use it

Only to run the app for you: to sign you in, to show your tasks and journal, to send the reminders you asked for, and to keep things in sync if you use the app offline. We don't use your content for advertising or profiling.

Where it is kept

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, in its Mumbai (India) region. Each person's rows are locked to their own account, so other users cannot read them. Data is sent over an encrypted connection. If you sign in with Google, Google handles that step under its own privacy policy and tells us only your name and email.

While you are offline, tasks and journal entries are kept on your phone and uploaded when you reconnect.

Permissions on your phone

  • Internet and network state: to sync and to know when you are offline.
  • Notifications, alarms and restart: to deliver task reminders and the daily journal reminders on time, including after your phone restarts. You can change the reminder times, or switch notifications off in your phone's settings.
  • Vibration: for reminder alerts.

The optional home-screen widget shows your check-in state and your next tasks. It reads from your phone and sends nothing anywhere.

Who we share it with

Only the services that make the app work: Supabase (storage and sign-in) and, if you choose it, Google (sign-in). We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account, we remove your profile, tasks, journal entries, history and statistics.

Your choices

  • Export: you can export your journal as a text file from the app.
  • Edit or remove: you can change or delete any task or journal entry yourself.
  • Delete your account: use the account deletion form on our Contact & Support page, sent from the email address you signed up with, and we will delete your account and its data.
  • Access or correction: write to us at the same address and we will help.

Children

DailyX is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has made an account, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how DailyX handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change matters to you, we will also tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy or your data: support@edantraeducation.com. For anything else, see Contact & Support.
